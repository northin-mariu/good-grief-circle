Thesis
Young parents don't want to plan a funeral, they want to be there for the big days anyway, so the whole product is one thing: write a letter, seal it, and know it will arrive on the day even if you can't.
Who it's for
Parents in their 30s and 40s with young children, after a quiet jolt: a child's birthday, a health scare, a friend's loss.
What's cut
Legal matters, Important Documents / vault, find help nearby, funeral and farewell options, songs and own song, will interview, Grief Support, Memorial, diagnosis path. About 80% of today's demo.
What's kept
Mari's Messages & Memories and Reflection questions (now used as writing prompts), choosing who and when, sealing, and the keyholder release (two of three confirm, with a wait the writer can cancel).
MVP for the Christmas vouchers
Write, seal and schedule letters on the web; three keyholders named by email; the voucher unlocks a free founding pack. Delivery itself is done by hand by Matt and Mari for years (nothing is due before 2027 anyway).
How it makes money
Writing is free. Delivery is a one-off Milestone Pack (draft: £39 for 12 letters, printed and posted with a wax seal on the day). Later: extra packs, and a gift version for grandparents.
The one metric
Share of new writers who seal their first letter within 14 days (target 40%).
The biggest risk
The promise runs 20+ years: people have to believe a 2026 start-up will still post Isla's letter in 2037. Needs a plain answer (printed copies held by keyholders, a named fallback) before anyone pays.
