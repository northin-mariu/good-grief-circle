Ten-Minute Funeral Wishes

Hide route cardShow route card
Thesis

Most people will never "start an end-of-life plan", but they will spend ten minutes writing down the funeral they want: do that one job beautifully, free, and sell the printed keepsake.

Who it's for

Anyone 40+ planning ahead, healthy, who has thought "I should tell them what I want" and searched for a funeral wishes template.

What's cut

Vault and documents, legal matters guide, will interview, circle and release, memorial, grief support, diagnosis path, directory, own song, song search, reflection, bucket list, messages, dashboard and accounts. About 85% of today's demo.

What's kept

Mari's ten Farewell & Ceremony and Personal Touches questions, one per screen; the "not a legal will" boundary; sharing with family.

MVP for the Christmas vouchers

One landing page, ten questions, a shareable card link. Each of the 100 vouchers carries a code for one free printed booklet, printed and posted by us by hand.

How it makes money

The card is free forever. A printed keepsake booklet, £24 one-off including two copies, extra copies £8 (draft prices). Later: funeral directors and hospices hand it out under their name.

The one metric

Share rate: % of finished cards sent to at least one family member within 7 days.

The biggest risk

Ten minutes is a one-visit product: if few buy the booklet, there is no second reason to come back, and paid ads cost more than £24 per buyer.
