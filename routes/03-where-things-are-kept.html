My will

What difference does a will make?

You choose who receives your money, property and belongings, name executors you trust, appoint guardians for children, and can leave gifts to charity. A clear will also makes probate faster and reduces the chance of family disputes.

Without it

Intestacy rules decide who inherits. Unmarried partners and stepchildren receive nothing automatically, and a court may need to decide who cares for your children.

Lasting Power of Attorney — Property & Financial

Lets someone you trust manage your money, bills and property if you can no longer do it yourself.

Your options

Without it

Banks can freeze access, even for a spouse. Your family would need to apply to the Court of Protection to become a deputy, which can take months and costs more.

Do it yourself online · Fill in the forms through the Office of the Public Guardian. Must be registered before it can be used.
With a solicitor · Helpful if your finances or family situation are complex, or if you want advice on choosing attorneys.
Joint or several attorneys · Attorneys can be required to decide together, or allowed to act independently. Each has trade-offs.
