Route 10
After Someone Dies
Thesis
When someone dies, the family needs one calm, ordered list with deadlines that they can split between them — and that's the whole product, free.
Who it's for
A family in the first days and weeks after a death, usually the adult child who ends up "doing the admin".
What's cut
All planning for yourself (only offered gently, six weeks on), the gift, the vault, keyholders, reflection, wishes & farewells, songs and own song, will interview, memorial, diagnosis path, pricing pages. About 80% of today's demo.
What's kept
Mari's first steps and help organisations, "find help nearby" (as funeral director listings), Grief Support, and a shared family task list.
MVP for the Christmas vouchers
No voucher needed: it's free. The 100 cards become "keep this for when you need it" cards. MVP = the list for England & Wales, sharing by link, a hand-picked set of local listings, the support page and one six-week email.
How it makes money
Free to families. Referral fees from funeral directors, probate services and will-writers, always labelled "we earn a fee" — never ranked by who pays.
The one metric
Lists shared with at least one other family member who takes a task within 7 days.
The biggest risk
Search is dominated by GOV.UK, the Co-op and funeral comparison sites; and referral money can look like profiting from grief if the labelling ever slips.
