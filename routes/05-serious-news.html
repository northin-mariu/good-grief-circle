Thesis
When the news is serious, a hospice hands the family one calm place: support first, then only the four essentials, in five-minute pieces a carer can fill in with the person’s say-so.
Who it’s for
Adults with a terminal diagnosis under a palliative or hospice team, and the partner or grown-up child who often holds the phone.
What’s cut
About three quarters of today’s demo: the gift, pricing pages, bucket list, reflection and goals, memorial, find help nearby, songs and own song, will interview, the documents vault, the Grief Support space, and every legal document except Health & Care LPA.
What’s kept
Mari’s support for a serious diagnosis and the helplines; Advance Care Wishes; Health & Care LPA guidance; three farewell questions; one message; plus a new one-page summary for the care team.
MVP for the Christmas vouchers
The 100 printed cards go to two or three friendly community palliative nurses, not to families. Each card carries a referral code the nurse hands over. Phone web app, no account needed, a printable summary. Every word clinician-reviewed before a single card is handed out.
How it makes money
Free for families. Hospices (later hospice charities and NHS palliative teams) pay a yearly licence per team. Price untested.
The one metric
Share of referred families whose care wishes and “who decides” reach their nurse within 14 days of the referral.
The biggest risk
Clinical and safeguarding: wording, mental capacity and a carer writing “with say-so” need clinician sign-off, and hospices buy slowly (governance, information sharing, small budgets).

Legally binding

Lasting Power of Attorney — Health & Care

Gives someone the authority to make decisions about your care and treatment when you cannot.

Without it: Doctors and social workers make decisions in your “best interests”. Family are consulted but have no legal right to decide, even a husband, wife or parent.

Do it yourself online: The same Office of the Public Guardian service as the financial LPA. Many people make both at once.

Have you got one?

Worth asking Joanne about: DNACPR & ReSPECT. Recorded with your doctor. A conversation about resuscitation and emergency care, best had early.
