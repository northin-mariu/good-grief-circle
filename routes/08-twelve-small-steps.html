Route 08 · Twelve Small StepsRoute card
Thesis
For people who mean to "sort it one day", replace the big planner with one five-minute step a week, answered by reply, and hand them a finished plan at week 12.
Who it's for
Procrastinators, 40 to 65, who know they should and never start. They sign up to the newsletter on goodgriefcircle.com, not to an app.
What's cut
The home dashboard and the three spaces; Planning in three steps; Grief Support; Memorial; diagnosis path; song search, soundtrack and own song; will interview; find help nearby; people to call; the documents vault; the gift screen; account and sync screens. There is no app to log in to: only "this week's step" and "what you've done".
What's kept
Mari's questions and guidance, cut into 12 small steps across four lanes: wishes, where things are kept, legal pointers, and messages. Plus the finished plan to share with one person.
MVP for the Christmas vouchers
The voucher code signs you up for 12 free weeks starting the first Sunday of January. One email (or text) a week from a fixed list of 12; replies read by us and added to the plan by hand if needed; a plan page at week 12. No accounts, no app.
How it makes money
A small yearly membership (draft: £19 a year through Stripe), asked at week 12: a seasonal "keep it current" check and a nudge after life changes. Stopping never hides the finished plan from you or the person you shared it with.
The one metric
Share of sign-ups who answer at least 8 of the 12 steps.
The biggest risk
Newsletter sign-ups are browsers, not buyers, and research says families paying yearly for planning rarely lasts. If people finish the 12 weeks happily but don't want year two, this is a lead magnet, not a business.

Why pensions matter, when you get to it

Most pension death benefits do not pass through your will. A nomination or "expression of wish" form tells the provider who you want to benefit.

You'll only ever get one of these per quiet week. If two go quiet in a row, we'll ask once if you'd like to pause.
