Ten designers each took one route and cut today's demo down to a single job. Each route below opens a clickable prototype: tap through it on your phone or in a narrow window.

These are concept pages, not the app. Each one invents its own person, so nothing comes from the Rita demo. Mari's wording is reused wherever a screen already existed; new wording is a draft, not Mari's voice. Every price is a draft placeholder, not a decision.

My recommendation

Build The Gift for Christmas, with The Keyholders inside it.

The Gift is the only route where the 100 printed vouchers are the product, so Christmas tests your one untested idea cheaply. Its promise ("Mum's wishes reach us when it matters") only holds with the two-of-three release from The Keyholders, which also settles the release question in your big four.

Before you choose

  • Several routes can stack. The Keyholders' release sits under 01, 03 and 06, and Ten-Minute Wishes could be the first screen of almost any of them.
  • The research backs 05, 07 and 10, where someone other than the family pays. Those buyers are slower than friends and family at Christmas.
  • 05 Serious News needs a clinician to review every word before any family sees it.
  • The end-of-October "big four" this helps with: the pricing model, the release mechanism and where the product lives. Company and roles are separate.
