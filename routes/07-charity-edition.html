Thesis
A charity pays a licence to give its supporters a free, short planning tool under its own name, because people who plan are the people who remember a charity in their will.
Who it's for
Healthy supporters of a UK charity (50–75, already giving), and the charity's legacy team who buy it.
What's cut
The gift, all payments and pricing, songs and own song, the diagnosis path, Grief Support, Memorial, reflection and goals, the will interview, the directory, people to call, memories, the circle release. About 75% of today's demo.
What's kept
A three-question funeral wishes set, eight "where it's kept" document notes, the will guidance (pointer only), Mari's "causes" question, a printable plan, and a partner dashboard of anonymous totals.
MVP for the Christmas vouchers
One friendly pilot charity. The 100 printed cards are co-branded with it and open the same five-step plan. The dashboard is a monthly totals sheet made by hand, not built yet.
How it makes money
A yearly licence per charity, sized by supporter list (figure to test in pilot talks). Free to every supporter, no upsell inside.
The one metric
Finished plans per 1,000 supporters emailed: the number a legacy manager takes to renewal.
The biggest risk
Charities want names for legacy follow-up; refusing to share them may make the licence hard to sell, and sharing them would break supporters' trust. Long charity buying cycles also miss Christmas.
Signature idea · the partner seat. The charity's colour gets exactly one seat: a circle docked beside the four logo circles, a thin ribbon at the top and its own newsletter and dashboard. Buttons, headlines and the glow always stay Good Grief Circle. Try another charity's colour on screen 7.

Step 3 · Your will

A will makes it count

Decides who inherits, who sorts out your estate, and who looks after children under 18.

Married or in a civil partnership

Your partner may still not inherit everything automatically. With a larger estate, part can pass to children under intestacy rules. A will makes your intentions clear.

SolicitorBest for property, blended families, trusts.
Online willQuick and affordable for straightforward estates.
Will-writing serviceCheck they belong to a professional body.
Free will schemesYou are invited, never required, to leave a gift.
Only if you'd like to

Are there causes you would like to support, now or in your will?

A gift in your will to charity can also reduce Inheritance Tax.

Whatever you choose, Harbour Hospice isn't told. It only appears in their totals as a number, never as you.
